Snooker rules (short)

  • Pot a red (1), then any colour (2–7), then a red again, and so on.
  • A potted colour goes back on its spot while reds remain.
  • When all 15 reds are gone, pot colours in order: Yellow 2, Green 3, Brown 4, Blue 5, Pink 6, Black 7.
  • Foul (wrong ball hit first, wrong ball potted, no ball hit, cue ball potted): opponent gets 4+ points and the turn.
  • Miss = turn passes. 40 seconds per shot.
  • Highest score at the end of the frame wins. Equal score = draw (stakes refunded).
  • Online: each player stakes KES 15. Winner receives KES 25. Only you and your opponent can see your table.
